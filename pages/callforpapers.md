The E3Vision-2026 invites original, unpublished research contributions from academia, industry, and research organizations across the domains of modern power and energy systems. The conference focuses on advanced power systems and grid stability, renewable energy conversion and net-zero technologies, power electronics and digital grid platforms, battery technologies and energy storage systems, and electric mobility, autonomous EVs, and smart energy management. The event aims to provide a premier interdisciplinary forum for researchers, practitioners, and policymakers to present recent advances, exchange technical insights, and discuss future directions toward sustainable, resilient, and intelligent energy and mobility ecosystems. All accepted and presented papers will be published in the E3S Web of Conferences, subject to successful peer review and publisher requirements.

---

### Track 1: Advanced Power Systems, Grid Stability & High-Voltage Engineering
- Power system planning, operation, and reliability  
- Grid stability, control, and resilience enhancement  
- Smart grids, microgrids, and active distribution networks  
- Power quality, harmonics, and protection systems  
- High-voltage AC/DC transmission and insulation engineering  
- FACTS, HVDC, and wide-area monitoring systems  

---

### Track 2: Renewable Energy Conversion, Control & Net-Zero Technologies
- Solar PV, wind, hydro, and hybrid renewable energy systems  
- Power converters and control strategies for renewables  
- Grid integration of inverter-based resources  
- Energy forecasting and net-zero transition technologies  
- Hydrogen energy systems and power-to-X technologies  
- Decarbonization and sustainable energy solutions  

---

### Track 3: Power Electronics & Digital Grid Platforms for Next-Gen Energy Systems
- Advanced power semiconductor devices (Si, SiC, GaN)  
- Multilevel inverters and high-efficiency converter topologies  
- Digital substations and IEC 61850-based systems  
- AI/ML applications in power electronics and grid control  
- Cyber-physical systems and digital grid security  
- Monitoring, diagnostics, and predictive maintenance  

---

**Track 4: Battery Technologies, Energy Storage & Supercapacitors**
- Lithium-ion, sodium-ion, solid-state, and emerging batteries  
- Battery modeling, diagnostics, and thermal management  
- Battery Management Systems (BMS) and state estimation  
- Supercapacitors and hybrid energy storage systems  
- Grid-scale and distributed energy storage applications  
- Energy storage for electric mobility  

---

#### Track 5: Electric Mobility, Autonomous EVs & Smart Energy Management
- Electric and hybrid vehicle architectures  
- Traction motors, drives, and powertrain optimization  
- EV charging infrastructure and fast-charging technologies  
- Wireless charging and V2G/V2X systems  
- Autonomous and connected electric vehicles  
- Smart energy management for mobility and microgrids  

---

Papers undergo a single-blind review process, by experts drawn from reputed organizations, across the world. Nontrivial, unpublished and original contributions will only be considered for the conference. The conference will have main papers, presentations, and poster presentations for short papers. All accepted and registered (presented) papers is planned to publish in the proceedings of **"E3S Web of Conferences”**.
